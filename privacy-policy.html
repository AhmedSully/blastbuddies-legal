Blast Buddies – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Blast Buddies (the "Game"), made by Bright Byte ("we", "us"), collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: blastbuddies.help@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Game progress, kept on your device. Your levels, stars, coins, Buddies, boosters and settings are saved on your device. We have no user accounts and no game servers that store your profile.

Anonymous gameplay analytics. To understand how the Game is played and to improve it, the Game sends gameplay events, for example: a level was started, won or lost; how many moves were left; a Buddy or booster was used; a purchase was made in the Game; when a play session started and how long it lasted. These events are linked to a random ID created by the Game when it is first opened. It is not your name, email, phone number or advertising identifier. We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) to receive and analyse these events on our behalf. Like any online service, GameAnalytics also receives technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and an approximate country derived from your IP address, and may use an app-specific device identifier provided by your phone. See gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store). We never receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded, and we record which item was bought.

Level updates. The Game may download updated level files when it starts. These requests contain no personal information; the hosting service may briefly log your IP address for security and delivery.

No advertising, no tracking. The Game currently shows no ads, does not collect your advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If this ever changes we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your permission first.

2. How we use it

To run the Game, balance its difficulty, fix bugs, understand which features players enjoy, measure how many players return, and test improvements (for example, comparing two versions of a level). We do not sell your information and do not use it to build advertising profiles.

3. Your choices

You can turn off anonymous analytics at any time in the Game: Settings → Share data. You can also ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your Game's random ID by emailing blastbuddies.help@gmail.com (use the Support button in Settings, which includes the ID automatically). Uninstalling the Game removes the progress stored on your device.

4. Children

Blast Buddies is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Legal basis and your rights

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the Game, and you can object at any time with the Settings switch above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict your information and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise them.

6. Retention and transfers

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and is then deleted or kept only in aggregated form. Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, with safeguards required by law.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version; important changes will be shown in the Game.
