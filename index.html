Blast Buddies – Support

Need help with Blast Buddies? Email us at blastbuddies.help@gmail.com. For the fastest answer, use the Support button in the Game's Settings: it adds your Game ID so we can find your issue.

Common questions

I bought something and didn't get it. Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, then restart the Game. If it is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I stop sharing gameplay data? Yes: Settings → Share data. See the Privacy Policy.
